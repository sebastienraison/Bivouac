Journal (new): log GPX tracks you've already hiked, single or multi-day. Chronological list by
year, read-only detail view with map and elevation profile, notes and tags with filtering,
multi-track overview on the map, duplicate a logged track into Planning to reuse it.

Planning: same three-stop detail drawer as the Journal, for a consistent interface across both.
The app reopens on whichever you last used; an incoming GPX file from another app asks which one
to open it in when that's not obvious.

Settings (new): custom speed for duration estimates (manual, automatic from your Journal, or from
a selection of tracks), a switch to disable non-free features (Esri satellite layer, Meteoblue
weather link), full backup/restore of your data.

Map: optional Esri satellite layer, direction arrows on the track (including loops).

Reliability: GPX content now lives in files instead of the database, removing a crash risk on
very dense or long tracks; fixed a bug where opening a track could fail right after a backup
until the app was restarted; updating from any previous release preserves all your data.
