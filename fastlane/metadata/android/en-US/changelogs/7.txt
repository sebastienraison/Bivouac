New: adjustable pause allowance in duration estimates (Settings → Custom speed) — adds a time
margin for breaks (photos, snacks, rest stops), either set manually or measured automatically from
the Journal/selection depending on the active mode.

Bugfixes:
- The automatic D+ (elevation gain) penalty could be overestimated when a stop was taken mid-climb
- Duplicating a Journal hike into Planning: the rename dialog could close itself before typing a name
- Planning: a multi-day trek duplicated from the Journal whose recording had stopped away from camp
  could show a fake path on the map and inflate the total distance shown
- A single-day track with no bivouac point had no way to export its GPX from Planning
- Opening a corrupted bank track showed a full error screen that hid the rest of the list; now
  shows a dismissible message instead
- Journal: bivouac row layout (time font, icon) aligned with the rest of the interface
